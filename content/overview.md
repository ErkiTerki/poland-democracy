---
title: Poland's democratic U-turn
question: Poland's liberal democracy score fell sharply under PiS (2015–2023) and has recovered since the October 2023 election. Which parts of democracy came back, which didn't, and do the indices agree?
---
## What we know so far
- **V-Dem liberal democracy index (v16, 2026):** 0.827 in 2010 → 0.417 in 2022 → 0.645 in 2025. Hungary kept falling over the same period (0.315 in 2025).
- **The recovery is uneven.** By 2025, freedom of expression (0.917) and legislative constraints on the executive (0.917) are back near pre-2015 levels. Judicial constraints are still 0.714, vs ~0.96 before 2015.
- **That matches the politics.** In Feb 2026, President Nawrocki vetoed the bill reforming the National Council of the Judiciary (KRS), and the coalition lacks the 3/5 majority to override. The next parliamentary election is in 2027.
- **Uncertainty matters.** V-Dem's 2024 report called the first (2023) uptick "small but statistically insignificant".
- **Turnout** in October 2023 was a post-1989 record (~74%).

## Rules from the brief
1. For every index: say what it counts as democracy, who judged it, and how wide the interval is.
2. For every "radical/populist" label: name the source and version (PopuList vs CHES), give the definition in plain language, and ship the classification table.
3. Keep description, prediction and causal claims separate. Country-level correlations say nothing about how individuals voted.
4. We need a project owner or audience. Candidates: Batory Foundation, Helsinki Foundation for Human Rights, OKO.press, Notes from Poland.
