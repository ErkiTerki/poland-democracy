Democracy Reporting International (Berlin). Its Digital Democracy team monitors and audits social media platforms and AI chatbots during European elections. It found 606 inauthentic TikTok accounts across Germany, Romania and Poland, which it calls “murky accounts” promoting parties and candidates. It also published an analysis of the 2025 Polish presidential campaigns on social media, covering over 5,000 posts.
