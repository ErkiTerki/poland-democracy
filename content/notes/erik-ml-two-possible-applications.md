## 1.
Train a model that tries to predict if an individual votes radical or not. We do this by combining the European Social Survey and PopuList or Chapel Hill Expert Survey, i.e. knowing where the political party an individual voted for is on the political spectrum. Then we use the couple of hundred answers of the ESS as the input variable. A good result would help us understand what has the greatest impact on whether an individual votes radical vs not.
