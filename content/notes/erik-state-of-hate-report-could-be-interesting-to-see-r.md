Publishes the annual State of Hate report. More relevant to you is its June 2026 report on gendered pathways to online radicalisation, which analysed over 11,000 interactions on X, Instagram and TikTok. It found that for some young men, fitness, gaming or political feeds lead into misogynistic and far-right content, while for some young women, “tradwife” and wellness content plays a similar role.
