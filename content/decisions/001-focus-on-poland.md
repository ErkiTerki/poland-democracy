**Decision:** Narrow the challenge to Poland.

**Why:** In V-Dem, most European countries declined over the last decade. Poland declined sharply (2015–2022) and then reversed after the 2023 election. That makes it a rare recovery case for testing which parts of democracy come back, and whether the indices agree.

**Alternatives considered:** A Europe-wide dashboard (too broad, and many teams will do it); a Hungary vs Poland comparison (keep it as a comparator).
