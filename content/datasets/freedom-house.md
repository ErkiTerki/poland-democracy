A separate democracy rating: political rights and civil liberties scored 0–100, with each country labelled Free, Partly Free or Not Free, since 1973. Download the Excel file "Country and Territory Ratings and Statuses" (one score per year) or "All Data" (detailed sub-scores, 2013 onwards). Free for academic use.
