**What it measures:** Harmonised versions of the main democracy indices (V-Dem, EIU, Freedom House, Polity, Lexical). Useful for the "do two indices agree?" question.

**How to download:** `https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/<chart-slug>.csv`, then filter to Poland. Example slugs: `liberal-democracy-index`, `judicial-constraints-on-the-executive-index`, `free-and-fair-elections-index`.

**Caveats:** The CSV gives central estimates only, without V-Dem's intervals.
