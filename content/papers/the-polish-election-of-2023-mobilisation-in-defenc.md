2023 Election not conducted with the “normal politics environment", concerned with the principles of constitutional democracy

“In response to this potentiality, numerous political commentators, along with Donald Tusk, exhorted vacillating voters to cast their ballots for this emergent coalition, regardless of any reservations they might harbour.”

Highly educated voters favored KO, the third way, and the left

Electoral map strongly reflects partitions of Poland
- Eastern poland → former russian partitions
- South eastern poland → former austrian partition
- Central western poland → former prussian partition
- Western territories → incorporated into poland after WW2

63% of individuals with primary education voted for PiS

Young voters → the confederation (18%), the Left (17%), Pis (14%)
