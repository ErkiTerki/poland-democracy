Does exposure to an algorithmically curated feed (vs chronological feed) affect users' political attitudes and behaviour? 

- Considerations: 

1) Switching off ≠ Switching on the algorithm: Meta in 2020 recorded that switching off the algorithm, before the presidential elections, lowered engagement rates but did not affect users' political attitudes. This does not mean that algorithm has no effect, but rather that users' opinions had already been primed by previous algorithm exposure. 

2) Social media are not homogeneous: the effect of algorithms might change based on platform.
