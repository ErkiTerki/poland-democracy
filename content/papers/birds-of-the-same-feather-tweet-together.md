How to estimate ideological position of regular people on Twitter?

- Three advantages of Twitter: 
1) We can measure ideological position of politicians and then measure users' appreciation for them through their "following" option
2) Abundance of data -> dynamic measure of ideology 
3) Name identification -> possibility of offline behaviour retrieval 

- Assumption: users prefer to follow people that are closer to them in the latent ideological space (avoid cognitive dissonance, allocate time optimally). 

- Statistical Model: user i decides whether to follow target user j on the basis of Euclidean distance between their ideal points, popularity ⍺_j and their political interest ß. To anchor the scale, we must fix the positions of at least two users (left and right wing, at -1 and +1). Political actors are identified on Twitter and followers of such actors are obtained.
