Results
23 videos, total over 4.5 million views, spreading allegations that the first round of presidential election in poland was rigged
majority of the videos posted in the week following the announcement of the results, 20/23
TikTok’s election label not consistent for all posts, IP dependent
hashtags such as #elections2025 and #elections do not seem to determine whether TikTok labels a post as election-related
might be a correlation between the candidate’s name in the hashtag and the presence of TikTok’s election label
Out of the total 675 posts, we found that 92 posts containing at least one candidate-related hashtag did not contain TikTok’s election label
TikTok still appears to be unable to effectively flag the majority of election-related content and content that undermines the electoral process
