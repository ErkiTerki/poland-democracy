Francesca Giannaccini one of the reporters ---> CONTACT

- Presence of murky accounts on Tiktok during electoral discorse
- Afd main beneficiary of these accounts
- Using manual data exploration and collection,  identified 138 murky accounts on TikTok impersonating and/or promoting major German parties or politicians. Of these, 68.8 percent promoted Adf (easiest to find, higher number of followers and created just before 2025 elections)
- The accounts were impersonating political parties rather than individuals
- Tiktok removed 112 accounts (after they reported them)
- Only a partial picture of the true extent of issues, given the inaccessible nature of TikTok’s  Virtual Compute Environment (VCE), the data access solution that TikTok offers to NGOs
ACTIVTY AND ENGAGMENT ANALYSIS:
- data taken manually 
- couldn't access to when created , so looked at dates of first video
- to assess reach of accounts looked at number of followers and total likes (from which got the average and total number of likes per party affiliations)
- specific period of interest (one month) in which they manually assess the extent of posting, reach, and engagement performance of videos
DATA COLLECTION
-  carried out through TikTok’s “Explore” section, where searched for the names of political candidates or parties.  Tried to identify all accounts that could be classified as “murky”, based on authenticity assessment criteria, described below. These accounts were carefully reviewed and flagged accordingly.  
CRITERIA
- analyse accounts username (and look for differences from official ones, like letters or numbers)
- profile pictures
- behavioural pattern (irregular activity)
- Verification status (if verified, not murky)
- Look at posts (monothematic..)
