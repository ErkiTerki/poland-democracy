After having identified the main, rough trend we wanted to do our research about, we decided to first seek guidance from possible product owners, rather than exploring completely by ourselves. We aim to follow their guidance and join their projects in the general field we're researching. This allows us to maximise our chance to find a product owners, and minimises risk to "get lost in the research", or to not find any owner.
