Our strategy is divided in three main parts: 

- Identifying ideal owners aligned with our scope: for now, candidates are Demagog, AI Forensics, Democracy Reporting International and Global Witness. 
- Building an ideal email to reach out to possible owners, in order to get a small coffee chat with them

Moreover, we have divided product owners in two main categories: 
- Scope 1: owners that are directly related to Social Media analysis, and which have developed some related tools - easier owners to attract
- Scope 2: other, maybe larger, owners that are working in the fields of European democracy or youth participation.
