title: In 2025 Poland is above the European average on all five V-Dem democracy indices, but only 21st–23rd of 40 European countries
type: description
source: V-Dem v16 (Coppedge et al. 2026); benchmark = unweighted mean of 39 European countries (V-Dem regions 1–4, without Russia, plus Cyprus)
owner: Francesco
date: 2026-10-29
---
- The average is pulled down by Belarus, Ukraine, Serbia, Hungary and others, so "above average" is not "top third". Read the rank, not only the gap.
- The European average itself fell after 2015 (Electoral democracy 0.764 → 0.718 in 2025), so part of Poland's closing gap comes from Europe falling, not only Poland rising.
