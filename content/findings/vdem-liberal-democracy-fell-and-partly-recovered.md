- 2016 is the largest one-year fall (−0.177); the 90% uncertainty intervals of 2015 and 2016 do not overlap, so the drop is beyond measurement noise.
- The 2022 low ranks Poland 34th of 40 European countries; by 2025 it is 22nd of 40.
- 2025 is still 0.130 below 2015: the recovery is partial.
- V-Dem scores are expert-based, constructed estimates, not observed facts. Another index (Freedom House, EIU) may rank Poland differently.
- Full series and European comparison: "Poland Analysis" tab of our V-Dem v16 workbook.
