There are examples of input variables we can use for the ML2 application:
- Freedom House (either total score or 7 subcategories)
- Turnout (IDEA): The turnout would be a few columns, e.g. 1. Turnout at last election; 2. Change from election before; 3. Years since last election; 4. Election this year (yes/no).
- Economic variables: GDP per capita; GDP growth; Inflation; Unemployment
- V-Dem's v2cacamps, on political polarization
- Populists or far-right in government (V-Party?)
- Public support for democracy: Claassen's "public mood" series
