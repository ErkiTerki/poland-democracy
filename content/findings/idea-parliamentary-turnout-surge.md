- From 1994 to 2018 Poland ranked 34th–39th of 35–40 countries every year (comparison: each country's most recent parliamentary election as of each year).
- 2023 was the first time Poland was above the European average (+11.3 points; average 63.1%).
- VAP turnout (votes ÷ all adults) rose less, from 49.4% to 67.9%, because fewer adults are on the voter roll than in the 1990s. Use one measure at a time when comparing countries.
