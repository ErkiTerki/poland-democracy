title: Since 1991, only two year-on-year changes in Poland's Electoral democracy score are larger than V-Dem's uncertainty: 2016 (−0.145) and 2024 (+0.132)
type: description
source: V-Dem v16 (Coppedge et al. 2026), Electoral democracy index v2x_polyarchy, 90% intervals (_codelow / _codehigh)
owner: Francesco
date: 2026-10-29
---
- Test used: a change counts as real only if this year's 90% interval does not overlap last year's.
- 2015 → 0.876, 2016 → 0.731, 2022 low 0.578, 2024 → 0.730, 2025 → 0.768.
- The same two years stand out on the other democracy indices. The timing matches changes of government (PiS took office Nov 2015, the Tusk coalition Dec 2023), but timing alone is not evidence of cause.
