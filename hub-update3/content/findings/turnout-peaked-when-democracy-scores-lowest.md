- This is a co-occurrence across 10 elections, not a correlation we can test and not evidence that backsliding caused higher turnout.
- A possible mechanism (polarisation and mobilisation against PiS, e.g. the 2020 abortion protests) needs individual-level data (ESS, Polish National Election Study) to check.
