- Measures whether people are denied public services, state jobs or equal civil liberties because of their political affiliation (partisan capture of the state).
- 2015 and 2022 90% intervals do not overlap ([0.012, 0.076] vs [0.136, 0.364]).
- Coverage ends in 2023 in V-Dem v16, so we cannot yet see whether this reversed after the change of government.
