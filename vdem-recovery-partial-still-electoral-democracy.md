- Share regained = (2025 − 2023) ÷ (2015 − 2023): Electoral 61%, Liberal 59%, Participatory 60%, Egalitarian 57%, Deliberative 84%.
- Regimes of the World: "Liberal democracy" every year 1993–2015, "Electoral democracy" 2016–2025.
- Regimes of the World is a classification built from V-Dem indicators (Lührmann et al.); category cut-offs are a coding decision.
